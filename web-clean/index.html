How to play

Cast — press and hold to swing the rod back (power meter climbs), release to send the lure flying.

Wait — watch the bobber. When a fish nudges it you'll get a STRIKE window — click fast.

Fight — the duel. Hold the mouse to reel (the blue distance bar drains — get the fish to the boat). The fish fights back: reeling raises strain (green→red bar). If strain maxes, the line SNAPS. Breathe (release) to let strain fall — but the fish gains water. Play chicken with the gauge.

Every catched creature gives XP. Level up to unlock richer waters. Rare species, junk items and maybe… something legendary.
